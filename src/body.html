Wake up inside the Serengeti.

Six canvas tents on a granite outcrop in the central plains. Twelve guests, one rock, and 14,750 km² of wilderness in every direction.

09:30 · The plains

1.3 million wildebeest. One long road.

The herds follow the rain in a loop through here. Our guides read the plains at dawn, so your first drive lands where the animals are, not where the brochure says.

11:45 · Up close

Close enough to hear them graze.

Zebra crop the tall grass first, and wildebeest follow for what is left. The plains feed everyone in order, and from the vehicle you can watch the queue.

Filmed on location. Tap the markers.
14:40 · The kopje

A rock older than the plains.

Kopjes are granite islands rising out of the grass. The Dutch word means “little head”. Lions sleep on them, and we built our tents on one.

15:55 · The path

The last stretch is on foot.

A gravel path, a storm lantern waiting on the trail and a ranger a few steps ahead. Each tent stands on its own raised deck, so the grass can do what it likes underneath.

Filmed on location. Tap the markers.
16:30 · Arrival

Six tents. No fences. One very good bath.

Leave your shoes on the deck. Your bags are already in the tent, the kettle is on, and the walk from the vehicle is thirty metres with a ranger at your side.

17:30 · Your tent

The last hour of light comes in sideways.

48 m² of canvas, teak and copper. A bed under a net, a bath you can fill facing the plains, and a deck where the sundowner arrives before you ask.

Look around: drag the scene, or tap a marker
18:40 · Sundowner

The sun goes down in one long minute.

At two degrees south, dusk lasts barely twenty minutes. Gin, rooibos or something stronger, on the rock, while the plain turns from gold to violet.

20:30 · The fire

Nothing here is loud, except the night.

Wood smoke, a hyena calling somewhere down the slope, and more stars than you have seen in one place. Your host tells the story of the rock. Then you sleep.
